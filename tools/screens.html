Screen-size check

This is a builder’s tool, not part of the platform — Vercel never uploads the tools/ folder. It loads the real application inside a frame you can resize, so the layout can be watched changing size by size.

360 × 740 phone layout

What to look for. At 360 px the sidebar is behind the menu button, tables are cards with labelled values, dialogs slide up from the bottom edge and buttons are finger-sized. At 768 px the same, one step roomier. At 1024 px the sidebar becomes an icon rail and two-column panels stay side by side. From 1366 px the full sidebar returns. On a very wide screen the content stops widening and centres, so text lines never stretch. The frame resizes the live app — no reload needed, except after changing the application’s own files.
